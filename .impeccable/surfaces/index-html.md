# Surface: metronome app (index.html)

Mode: Operate. The whole app is this one screen; settings live below the playing area.

## Brief

- Audience and job: students and hobbyists practising alone; the phone sits on a stand at arm's length and is read with a glance while both hands play.
- Success: the beat position is followed in peripheral vision (strong beat motion), the tempo is readable from arm's length, and tempo or meter change in one or two touches between passages.
- Must not: distract from practice (no flashing, no busy animation), read as a generic app template, or crowd controls and shrink type.
- Untouched: every feature, shortcut and setting in PRODUCT.md; the icon is the brand reference.
- Memorable moment: the baton point falling into each beat point exactly on the click.
- Open: none.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The bar as a conductor draws it: a baton point traces the meter's beat pattern and strikes each ictus on the click. Refuses the big-number-and-dot-row app.

OWN-WORLD: Music-room chalkboard and conducting textbook: icon-navy slate (cool paper in light) owns the surface; chalk-white strokes and numerals; one yellow for baton and accents; tempo set as a score's mark, "Allegro ♩ = 120".

STORY: The player follows the bar from the corner of the eye, trusts the strike, and changes tempo or meter in one or two touches.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Pattern figure fills the upper half, its numerals the accent buttons; below, the tempo mark at glance size between − and +, a ruler engraved with Italian markings, then Start flanked by Tap and Trainer.

FORM: Conductor's beat-pattern diagram, #1 on my list (user-chosen pick), seed key 44a0d72a (degraded roll).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
